# Morning Routine 🌞

*During your first couple of days, you'll start with putting together your system, and getting necessary applications, like email and Slack, set up.*
 
----

Generally, you can start each day by running through this list of tasks:

1. **Perform updates:** open a terminal and run `sudo yum update`.
2. **Log in to Slack:** Scan the "general" channel to stay updated with daily happenings. Keep your eye on the "intern-projects" channel to help out with various tasks as needed.
3. **Check your email:** At the end of each week, make sure to email Scott with a bulleted list of tasks you have been working on or topics you have been studying throughout the week. 
4. **Check the calander** for meetings or events.
